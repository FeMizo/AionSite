## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the school's own painted wall (barda rotulada): hand-lettered block type on whitewash, flat painted color fields and a green guardapolvo with a red edge. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed photo under a dark veil with a centered headline and a row of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Whitewash ground (#FBFAF6 with faint plaster mottling), painted fields in deep green #14532A and #1F7A35, signal red #E02028 for action and the guardapolvo edge, carbón #3A3A3C lettering. Wide block lettering (Archivo, wdth 112–125, 800–900) painted at mural scale. Photos hang as painted panels with a thin green frame. Square-cornered painted shapes (radius ≤ 6px); the only curve is the logo's "M" arc.

STORY: Parents see a real, proud local school, read that its students win (Alondra, 1st place state Olimpiada; Andrés, Oratoria and Legislatura Infantil), understand the levels (Preescolar, Primaria) and an inclusive culture, then tap "Agendar visita".
